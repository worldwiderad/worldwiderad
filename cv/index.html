Modelling the Other

Whether a frontier model predicting a named peer model uses evidence about that peer or a prior tied to who built it. Sole author, advised by Sungjoo Ha and Min Lin. I wrote the whole pipeline: collection from sixteen subject models across several providers, dual-judge labelling, the prediction runner and the analysis. A pilot has run; the design is in blind review before pre-registration. Submission to TMLR planned for December 2026 or January 2027.
