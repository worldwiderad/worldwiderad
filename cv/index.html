Research and writing

from October 2026

Learning what to prove

My independent research asks whether a learned model, cheap to run at each step, can choose what an automated theorem prover explores next. The evaluation framework is built, with proofs checked in Lean, and I’m testing its repeatability. There is no evidence yet from this work that a learned policy helps.

June 2026 to now

Modelling the Other

My study asks whether a frontier model predicting a named peer model uses evidence about that peer or a prior tied to who built it. I’m the sole author, advised by Sungjoo Ha and Min Lin. The design has been piloted, and the ground-truth collection code has been validated and frozen. Live end-to-end dry runs are under way before preregistration and the main study, so there are no confirmatory findings yet. I’m aiming for TMLR after the main study.
