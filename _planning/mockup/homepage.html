Modelling the Other

This started in June 2026 with a question about language models. When one model predicts how a named peer will behave, is it using evidence about that peer, or a prior about where the peer was built, such as the US or China? I’m the only author.

I’ve run a pilot. Before pre-registering, I’m putting the design through rounds of blind review by up to 21 model instances and checking every flaw they claim by simulation.

Pre-registration next, then TMLR in December or January.

Orchestral music

I compose in MuseScore and produce in Logic Pro, mostly for orchestra, occasionally with synths. My MYP Personal Project was a symphony in one movement, rated Excellent. The biggest thing I’ve written is about ten minutes long and runs to 49 tracks: eleven woodwind parts including bass oboe and bass clarinet, sixteen brass including six horns, strings, percussion, harp, piano and synth. Recordings are on SoundCloud.

Working towards a multi-movement symphony that can be played live.
